A **conversation** lasts: a subject, its messages, and the people and agents taking part. Work starts in a conversation and reports back to it.

| Kind | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Chat | A private conversation with Smithers and your agents. |
| Issue discussion | Comments on an [issue](/docs/how-it-works/issues/), visible to the repository's team. |

## Chats

**Available now.** [Chat](/docs/learn/chat/) with Smithers in the app or the TUI. The app keeps your chats for your account; the TUI keeps conversations in the project.

**Partly available.** The API keeps private chats, searchable and syncable, with agents replying under their own names. The app's chat list is planned ([#2111](https://github.com/smithersai/smithers/issues/2111)).

## Issue discussions

**Available now.** Every issue has comments, and every change to it is recorded. The list shows the repository's Smithers issues with its GitHub issues.

## Messages start work

A message can trigger a flow. The assistant answers, or hands the request to the right [agent](/docs/how-it-works/agents/) and posts its reply in the same conversation.

**Planned.** Message triggers for chats. See [Triggers and flows](/docs/how-it-works/triggers-and-flows/).
