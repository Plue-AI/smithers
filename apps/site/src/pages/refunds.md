**Rule:** if you ask within 7 days of your first Pro charge, we refund that charge in full.

- Email [support@smithers.sh](mailto:support@smithers.sh) from your account's email address, or with your Smithers account name, within 7 days of the first charge.
- We refund to the original card through Stripe. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.
- A refund ends Pro and removes its unused model credit.
- After the first 7 days, and for later monthly renewals, charges are not refunded, including unused days. Cancel anytime in the billing portal to stop the next renewal; Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
- If we charge you in error, or end your paid plan without cause, we refund it.
