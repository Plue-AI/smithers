# C-J10-06 Sync health: "synced Ns ago", gold past 120 s on network loss, Retry

Proves: mvp.md J10.6, §6.3 "Sync status" · spec.md §4.4, §6.2.3, §12.2.3, §12.6, §14.3 (Home `main`) · Layer: e2e · Stage: S1 · Tickets: T-GH-07
Automation: `apps/app/e2e/real/github-j10/sync-health.spec.ts` (new) · Runs in: reference host

## Setup
- The T-GH-08 `github_sync_health_integration_test.go` supplies production live-channel tests with polling disabled, stale timer restart recovery and zero-request Retry pauses before the browser journey.

- Reference host, install at the commit under test, App installed on a fresh `smithers-mvp-canary/<date>`. Webhooks inactive.
- The Home card open in a browser on a second Mac at the install's LAN origin, signed in as Alice (Member). The owner signed in on another browser.
- A `pf` anchor on the reference host, off at start, that blocks `github.com` and `api.github.com` (address ranges from `https://api.github.com/meta`) and leaves the LAN open.
- The owner's GitHub session can suspend the App's installation.
- A recorder of the `home` topic's frames in Alice's browser.

## Steps

- Decode and render all four main health fixtures. Assert one github.retry registration per registry and one dispatch per press. A successful poll projects health fresh; synced is display copy, not a health value.
- Invoke `/github` and the external-agent skill against a recording transport; press Home Retry and ask the app agent to retry. Inspect the production catalog dispatch and both install and Plue route compositions.

- Adopted T-GH-08 boundary cases: Disable polling, subscribe through production /api/live, cross 120 s since success and observe one stale delta. Restart before the threshold and repeat without polling to prove timer recovery. During persisted Retry-After and exhausted budget, repeatedly press Retry through production routes and independently assert zero upstream requests; once admitted, scheduling can fetch within 1 s

1. Sample the `main` row's text and colour every 1 s for 5 min. Record every `home` delta and `GET /api/github/sync`.
2. Enable the block at t_b. Keep sampling every 1 s. Record `last_success_at` (`S`) from `GET /api/github/sync`.
3. At t_b + 6 min, record the row.
4. Alice presses **Retry** while blocked.
5. Disable the block. Alice asks the app agent "retry the GitHub sync".
6. Suspend the App's installation on GitHub. Wait 2 min. Record the row and `GET /api/github/sync`. Unsuspend and press **Retry**.

## Pass when

- The adopted owner contract passes: Decode and render all four main health fixtures. Assert one github.retry registration per registry and one dispatch per press. A successful poll projects health fresh; synced is display copy, not a health value.
- `/github` and its skill issue GET only. Retry dispatches the separate `github.retry` in-card descriptor with `agent: run` and Member minimum role through `cardActions` → `flowAction`; both member-session and delegated requests execute without confirmation. Install removes legacy main-pull routes; Plue retains its routes/workers. Rate-limit and budget assertions above still apply.

- Retry immediately schedules all streams subject to their persisted retry_at and shared budget admission. It sends no upstream request during a 429 pause or exhausted budget. Persist success/health inputs and recover the independent stale-boundary timer on startup; crossing 120 s publishes a stale home delta without a poll. Remove old main-pull routes only from install composition and preserve Plue routes/workers

- `GET /api/github/sync` returns `{state, last_success_at, cause?, retry_at?}` and no precomputed age (§12.6).
- Step 1: the row reads "synced Ns ago" with N ≤ 60 at every sample and is never gold. N equals now − `last_success_at` within 2 s, and the number advances every second while the recorder shows no `home` delta for that second.
- Step 2: the row turns gold at the first sample after `S` + 120 s, within 2 s, and is never gold before `S` + 120 s.
- Step 3: the row reads "synced 6 min ago · Retry" (±1 min).
- Step 4: Alice, a Member, may press Retry (§12.6). It is acknowledged at once, chat stays usable, and the row stays gold.
- Step 5: the app agent runs Retry at once with no confirmation (`agent: run`), and the row is fresh within 10 s.
- Step 6: the state is `refused`, and the row names the App installation as the cause with a link to the Settings card. After unsuspend and **Retry**, it is fresh within 10 s.

## Fail when
- The row turns gold before 120 s, or claims "synced" while GitHub is unreachable.
- The server sends a `home` delta every second to move the age.
- **Retry** blocks chat, shows done before a stream succeeds, or is refused to a member or posts a confirmation.
- A refusal shows as plain stale with no cause.
