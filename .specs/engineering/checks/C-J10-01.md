- Members: the owner, and Ben (Maintainer) in a browser on a second Mac. Model access set. Capacity ≥ 2 on the detected host.

## Steps
1. Ben appends T1 "Add retry helper". Wait for In review.
2. Ben appends T2 "Retry webhooks" with acceptance "retries 3 times with backoff". Wait for In review.
3. Read T2's PR with `GET /repos/{o}/{r}/pulls/{n}` and its head commit.
4. Open T2's TODO card and its PR diff in the browser.
5. Ben amends T2 with "also log each retry" (+1). Wait for the verified update.
6. The owner merges T1 in Smithers. Wait for T2's update.

## Pass when
- T2's PR title is "Retry webhooks", its head ref is `smithers/retry-webhooks`, its base is `main`, and the App opened it.
- T2's head commit has one parent, the `main` tip at proposal time, and its tree holds T1's and T2's changes.
- The body holds: revision 1's prompt, the acceptance text, a checks table with at least one row, a diff stat, the review summary, T1 named as included and linked to T1's PR, a link to the TODO at `<origin>`, and "Requested by @ben".
- The body has no closing keyword (`fixes`, `closes`, `resolves` before an issue reference).
- Smithers' diff for T2 lists none of T1's paths.
- After step 5, the same PR number shows revision 2's prompt and not revision 1's.
- After step 6, T2's head is a new commit whose parent is the new `main` tip, and the body no longer names T1.

## Fail when
- The PR is based on T1's branch, or the branch is `smithers/issue-N` or `smithers/change-…`.
- An amend or a rebase opens a second PR.
- The body names a merged item, or an included item renders as a bare `#n` that GitHub autolinks to an unrelated issue or PR.
- Smithers' diff includes T1's files.
