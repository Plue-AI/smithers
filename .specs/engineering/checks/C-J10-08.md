# C-J10-08 PR closed on GitHub turns the TODO Dropped with its actor; reopen restores it

Proves: mvp.md §6.3 "A TODO's PR is closed without merging", M-22 · spec.md §4.1 (any unmerged → dropped, dropped → in_review), §10.2.3, §12.3 (closed row) · Layer: integration · Stage: S1 · Tickets: T-GH-05
Automation: `packages/backend/internal/services/github_inbound_pulls_integration_test.go` (new) · Runs in: CI

## Setup
- Real PostgreSQL and real git. `githubfake` with three TODO PRs, for T1, T2 and T3 In review in that stack order, and its write log.
- The scheduler under an injected clock.

## Steps
1. On the fake, user `alice` closes T2's PR without merging (`closed_by: alice`).
2. Advance one pulls cycle (60 s).
3. Deliver the same close again: a second poll result and a webhook hint.
4. At day 6 after the close, reopen T2's PR on the fake. Advance one cycle.
5. Close T3's PR as `alice`, advance one cycle, then reopen T3's PR at day 8 and advance one cycle.

## Pass when
- After step 2: T2 is `dropped` with `state_reason` "closed on GitHub by @alice"; one `todo_events` row carries Alice's GitHub actor; T3 has `rebase_pending`, and its next candidate no longer includes T2.
- After step 2, the write log has no Smithers write for T2 (no second close and no "Dropped in Smithers" comment).
- Step 3 adds no event and no state change.
- After step 4: T2 is `in_review` with one new event, back at its previous position between T1 and T3, its PR is followed again, and `smithers/<slug>` on the fake points at T2's last verified candidate.
- After step 5: T3 is `dropped` after the close and stays `dropped` after the day-8 reopen.

## Fail when
- The TODO shows `rejected`, `blocked` or `failed` instead of Dropped.
- The actor is missing or shows Smithers.
- A reopen within 7 days is ignored, lands at the end of the stack while its position is free, or one after 7 days restores the TODO.
- A repeated close produces a second transition.

## Evidence
`.artifacts/checks/C-J10-08/<UTC timestamp>/`: `todos.json` and `todo_events.json` after each step, the fake's `writes.jsonl`, the test log and the commit.
