01

Layers: Paper tokens up to catalog commands, and who owns each

#layers

02

The View/Container seam: data comes in as props, gestures leave as catalog tags

#seam
  • A missing action means no button. The Container filters actions[] for the viewer; the View never decides who may act.
  • Tags are opaque. The View hands back action.tag, and the control carries data-flow={action.tag}.
  • A field the View needs but the props lack is a spec change. Raise it with the tech lead (8a), who updates spec.md §14.3, ui-components.md and T-APP-19 together.

03

Shell anatomy: one conversation per branch, a timeline rail on desktop, pills on narrow screens

#shell
  • Everything embeds in the conversation. Nothing opens full screen unless the person maximizes it (EMBED LAW).
  • The timeline is desktop only. Hiding a notice keeps its timeline entry and its action.
  • UI-only flows (theme, open, maximize, navigate, filters) change only the screen of the person who ran them, even when their app agent ran them.

Source mvp.md §6.4 (Home card, Branch conversations, Toasts, Timeline, Browser notifications), M-08, Appendix B.1; spec.md §14.1.1–§14.1.4, §14.4, §14.5.1, §14.5.3, §14.5.4, §7.2.2; apps/app/AGENTS.md (EMBED LAW, onboarding brief on the composer); mock AppFrame.tsx, Rail.tsx, Tree.tsx, mock.css (PORT · the left rail).

04

Card anatomy: header, body, one actions row, and one card per thing

#card
  • One solid button per card, for its primary act. Every other button is outline or ghost.
  • A reason replaces the button it blocks, as text. An Action with disabled.reason renders that way.
  • Card body lines hold 12 words or fewer, in product words only.

05

Identity: every participant has one mark, wherever it appears

#identity
  • People are circles; agents are rounded squares; Smithers is the ink square.
  • Smithers is one participant, the app agent and the stack service alike. Acting for a person it reads “Smithers for Ben” and wears Ben's dot, never Ben's avatar with a badge.
  • A mark's color is identity: the person's lane, also for agents acting as them; teal for the coding agent; ink for Smithers.

Source mvp.md §3.1, §6.8 (Presence, External changes), M-21, M-27, M-32, M-34; spec.md §2 (actor notation), §7.3.2, §14.6a; ui-components.md Actor, T-UI-01; mock parts.tsx (Avatar, actorName, flagName, identityColour), world.ts (isSmithers, forWhom, via), mock.css (PORT · avatars), css/code.css (flags).

06

Color semantics: one meaning per color, and actor colors apart from status colors

#color
  • One meaning per color: teal live or act, gold a person is needed, ember failed. Everything settled or automatic is neutral.
  • Actor colors say who, never what state, and a status never borrows a lane color.
  • Both themes gate acceptance: every swatch has a light and a dark value.

Source design README (Visual rules); mvp.md §6.3 (Sync status), §6.4 (Timeline), §6.12 (Pinned versions), §12; spec.md §4.4, §14.5.2; apps/app/src/mainview/styles/tokens.css; mock mock.css (PORT · identity and state tokens, PORT · TODO state, “Teal means live or act” overrides).

07

TODO lifecycle: nine states, each with one glyph and one pill

#states
  • Each state has one glyph and one pill, the same on every card, row and timeline line.
  • Merged is done. Learning is a receipt on the merged card (“2 lessons”), never a state.
  • Drawn from mvp.md §4.1 and spec.md §4.1. spec.md's transition table is authoritative and adds rarer edges: Discard returns to In review, a reopened PR revives a Dropped TODO, and a merge on GitHub counts from any unmerged state.

Source mvp.md §4.1, M-15; spec.md §4.1 (with §4.1.0a precedence and §4.1.1 queue copy), §10.7, §10.8; mock parts.tsx (StateGlyph, StatePill, stateLabel), mock.css (PORT · TODO state).

08

One attempt is one durable run: five steps, then a wait for merge

#run
  • One attempt is one durable run of its pinned flow version. It lives until the TODO merges or is dropped; Retry starts a new attempt beside the old one.
  • After Propose the run holds at the wait for merge. A rebase loops it back to Verify, a steer or a requested change to Implement.
  • Phase titles and cell labels are deterministic. ✦ marks what the fast model wrote, only for runs someone inspects.

09

Merge readiness: one decision, read by every merge surface

#merge-rule
  • One rule for every merge surface: no card offers a merge another card would refuse.
  • The tests run in the order a person fixes them. The first failing test's reason replaces Merge, as text.
  • A new revision reruns checks and review, keeps the old review as history, and clears an earlier approval.

Source mvp.md §4.2 (Rebase, Merging), §6.3 (Checks finish, GitHub branch protection), §6.10, M-05; spec.md §10.6.1, §10.6.2, §10.6.2a, §10.6.2c, §10.5.3; mock world.ts (mergeReadiness, revise, checksPassed), cards/Home.tsx, cards/Todo.tsx (Evidence), cards/Confirm.tsx. Engineering view ↗

10

App agent permissions: runs at once, asks once, opens your merge, or has no path

#permissions
  • The app agent acts with the asker's authority and shows as Smithers for that person. It never merges or approves.
  • External agents through the CLI or skill follow the same run, confirm and never rules.
  • A flow has one card, whoever runs it. Only the actor's glyph tells a person's act from an agent's.
